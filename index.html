The Sound

Music has always been the constant. Years ago that meant running a site for remixes, uploading tracks one by one for anyone who wanted to hear them. Bezsen808 picks up that same thread — heavily saturated 808s pushed past clean, drums stripped down to almost nothing, vocals that sound ripped from a phone speaker on purpose.

Every track is built from scratch in FL Studio, by hand, start to finish. Nothing ships untouched.

Mastered to streaming spec: −14 LUFS integrated, −1 dBTP ceiling. Loud enough to hit, clean enough to pass.
